Delete your Wing account

This page is for the Wing app (Android package and iOS bundle au.com.wingapp.wing), published on Google Play by Wing Connect (Paradise Concepts Pty Ltd, trading as Wing). You can delete your account and everything attached to it at any time, without contacting anyone. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1: in the Wing app

  1. Open Wing and go to the You tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

You are signed out and your account is deleted as soon as you confirm.

Option 2: in a web browser, without the app installed

  1. Go to wing.events in any browser.
  2. Sign in with the Australian mobile number you used for Wing. A 6 digit code is sent to you by SMS.
  3. Open your Profile, expand Privacy & Safety, and choose Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

This uses the same account and the same deletion as the app. If you no longer have access to the mobile number, use Option 3.

Option 3: ask us to delete it for you

Email founder@wingapp.com.au from any address with the subject Delete my Wing account and the mobile number the account was created with. We confirm the number belongs to you, delete the account, and reply to say it is done. Requests are actioned within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in: mobile number, first name, date of birth, and optional email address.
  • Your profile and profile photo, including bio, profession, pronouns, links, and every profile preference.
  • Your venue check-in history and any active session.
  • Your connections, connection requests, and every message you sent or received.
  • Your notifications, push notification registrations, and any sign-in tokens held by the app.
  • Any consent you gave to share your email address with a venue, and any queued venue SMS to you.

Deletion happens at the moment you confirm. There is no holding period and no way to restore the account afterwards. If you return to Wing you start again as a new user.

What is kept, and why

  • Safety reports. If you reported someone, or someone reported you, the report record is kept so that venue and Wing safety records survive account deletion. Your name, contact details, and any identifying fields you added to a report are removed from it at the same time your account is deleted. No follow-up with you is possible after that.
  • Venue email suppression. If you had chosen to share your email address with a venue, a one-way hash of that address (not the address itself, and not linked to you) is kept so the venue removes you from its list and does not email you again.
